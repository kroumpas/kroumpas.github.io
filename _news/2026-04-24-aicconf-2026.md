We presented [_Modelling Eye Mechanics Using Eye-tracking Glasses and Deep Learning_](https://doi.org/10.1109/AICCONF69182.2026.11600664) at AICCONF 2026 in Prague. A pure physics-informed neural network predicted gaze better than graph, temporal and hybrid networks.

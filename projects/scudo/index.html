SCUDO

Horizon Europe · operator dashboards for disruption-resilient transport

SCUDO (Safe and Comprehensive Urban Development and Transport Management for Disruption-resilient Operations) is a Horizon Europe research and innovation action on keeping transport networks safe and running through disruption, with use cases in Athens, Bavaria, Amsterdam and Venice. I design the project’s operator dashboards at Frontier Innovations, one of the partners.

Venice: lagoon operations

When an acqua alta event hits Venice, an operator has to reason about tide forecasts, closed pontili, suspended waterbus routes, and stalled waste collection all at once. The design problem was triage: how do you let one person read a fast-moving, multi-source situation in seconds and know what to do next? I led with a single “Situation Overview” that pins the active disruption and its severity to the top, then lays out operational summary, network status, live alerts, a 12-hour tide/wind/demand forecast, and impact KPIs in a scannable grid — with prioritised recommended actions so the interface doesn’t just report, it prompts a decision.

SCUDO situation-overview dashboard for the Venice lagoon transport and waste-collection network: disruption banner, forecast, impact KPIs, and ranked response options in one view.

Athens: metro disruption

The same layout carries over to the Athens metropolitan transport network, here for an extreme-rainfall event: closed stations and affected lines on the map, line status and live alerts beside it, a 1–6 hour forecast, and the impact KPIs and ranked recommended actions underneath.

SCUDO situation overview for the Athens metropolitan transport network during a flooding scenario: station closures on the map, line status, alerts, forecast, impact KPIs and recommended actions.

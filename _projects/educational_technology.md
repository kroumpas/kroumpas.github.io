This is where my research meets the classroom. I study and build adaptive learning systems that draw on multimodal data — eye-tracking, EEG, and behavioural signals — to understand what learners actually need, and I design the tools that put those ideas in educators' hands.

A large strand of this work lives on the Minecraft Education platform: designing curriculum-aligned educational games and courses, and building AI-assisted tools that let teachers create their own courses without being developers {% cite roumpas2024platform %}. That practical work fed directly into published research on bridging the gap between educators and game-based learning {% cite roumpas2025minecraft %}. Much of it sits within EU-funded (Erasmus+ and Horizon) projects.

**What's here:** game-based learning design, AI-assisted course creation, multimodal learning analytics, educator training.

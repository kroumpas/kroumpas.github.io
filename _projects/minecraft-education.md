Alongside [CourseCrafter](/projects/coursecrafter/), I design and build the Minecraft Education content for EU-funded education projects, most of them
Erasmus+: the worlds, missions and lessons that carry each project's subject into the game, from car mechanics to astronomy, nanotechnology, AI and food
waste. Rather than give each its own page, they're collected here.

## projects

- **[CarCraft](https://carcraft-project.eu/)**: a Minecraft-based simulation game for vocational students training as car mechanics.
- **[BAFOS](https://bafos.eu/)** (_Boosting school Awareness about FOod loSs_): students explore the causes and consequences of food loss and waste in a
  FoodWaste world with game missions.
- **[AI-COSMIC](https://ai-cosmic.eu/)** (_Minecraft for AI and Coding Introduction in Primary Schools_): AI concepts and basic coding for primary pupils,
  through challenges that need simple code to solve.
- **[AstronoMine](https://astronomine.erasmusplus.website/)**: raising interest in astronomy among young students and primary teachers.
- **[NANOWARE](https://nanoware.erasmusplus.website/)**: nanotechnology awareness for secondary-school science teachers and their students.
- **Follow Me to Minecraft**: English at CEFR level A1 for young learners, with a teacher's guide for class and home.
- **EPITOME**
- **[Treasure Hunt: PEMDAS](https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/lessons/treasure-hunt-pemdas)**: an order-of-operations maths lesson in the official
  Minecraft Education library: a pirate crew, an island and six treasures.

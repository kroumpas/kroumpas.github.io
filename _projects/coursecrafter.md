## what it is

<!-- TODO: confirm. -->

CourseCrafter lets teachers build a Minecraft lesson without writing commands. They lay out the world on a grid, add non-player characters and write their dialogue, set up scores that change with students' choices, add titles and subtitles, and upload their own material or start from a text prompt.
